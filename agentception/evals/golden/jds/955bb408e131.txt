Together, we’ve provided more than  3 million hours of healthcare, helping Veterinarians earn more than $200 million. About the Role
We're in the builder era. Prompt-to-feature workflows are real, AI coding tools are the new norm, and the teams that win will be the ones who set up the right infrastructure to move fast with them.
We're hiring an engineer to own the frontend platform at Roo. That includes our design system, shared UI architecture, developer tooling, AI-assisted workflows, and the technical foundation that helps every team member build polished, consistent product experiences.
You're creating the workflows that unlock capacity for the whole team. You'll identify and build the highest-leverage improvements to our frontend platform, from design system tooling and AI integrations to shared infrastructure, developer experience, and platform modernization. Your customers are every builder at Roo—engineers, designers, and AI agents. Success in this role means making it dramatically easier for all of them to build great product experiences.
This is a high-agency, find-it-fix-it-ship-it role. You'll identify the problems, scope the solutions, and build them, without needing a full squad behind you. The right person is already using AI tools to move faster than anyone around them and sees this as a chance to do that at platform scale.
Responsibilities
Own the long-term technical direction of Roo's frontend platform, including the systems, tooling, and architecture that power development across web and mobile.
Partner with Product and Design to evolve our design system as a foundational platform rather than a standalone library.
Make our design system effortless to consume by engineers, designers, and AI agents through thoughtful integrations, documentation, tooling, and developer workflows.
Lead platform initiatives like framework upgrades, React Native modernization, Expo adoption, and shared library evolution.
Own the migration of legacy components to our v2 design system — including the tooling and documentation that let the rest of the team complete the migration themselves.
Establish patterns that let engineers ship new pages and flows quickly from a component library that's built for how people actually build in 2026.
Improve developer experience through shared tooling, automation, documentation, templates, and workflows that increase engineering velocity.
Identify and eliminate the friction points that slow builders down — missing tokens, undocumented patterns, inconsistencies that force one-off decisions — and fix them systematically.
