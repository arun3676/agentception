Straight answers

What the public
product does today.

Where do the jobs come from?

Agentception uses configured discovery providers. A result links to the returned source when the URL is present and safe to open.

What happens when search is unavailable?

The product should show an error or unavailable state. It should not substitute a fabricated listing or treat a transport failure as a successful search.

Do I need to upload a résumé?

No. Public search does not accept a résumé. Résumé upload and other personal workflows are unavailable while authentication and ownership controls are rebuilt.

Can I save, tailor, or track an application?

Not in the current public product. Those actions require a secure account and remain unavailable during containment.
