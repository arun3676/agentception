Where do the jobs come from?

Agentception searches direct ATS postings, including Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, and Workable, then filters and ranks the results against your target and resume evidence.

Is the learning path generic?

No. It uses your topic, level, learning style, goals, and time commitment, and can connect the result to gaps found in the target role.

How does the outcome loop work?

Log what happened after each application. Once there is enough personal evidence, Agentception can surface patterns instead of pretending one application proves anything.

Do I need to upload a resume?

No. You can search without one, but resume evidence makes matching, gap analysis, tailoring, and targeted study much more useful.
