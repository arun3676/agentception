# Agentception market and UX research — July 2026

## Executive read

The 2026 market is no longer missing AI job search, resume matching, application tracking, or personalized learning in isolation. LinkedIn and Indeed now explain job fit and support natural-language discovery; Teal and Simplify connect resumes with job tracking and application workflow; roadmap.sh and Coursera connect skills with structured learning. The opportunity is therefore not to claim those categories do nothing. It is to make the handoff between them materially better: current role evidence should select one important gap, that gap should become a scoped learning-and-project plan, and application outcomes should update the next move. Agentception already has the beginnings of that connected loop in one codebase.

## What leading products now cover

| Product | Current strength | Remaining opening for Agentception |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn Jobs | Natural-language job search, qualification match summaries, missing qualifications, profile and resume signals | Make the missing qualification immediately actionable as a scoped project and feed personal outcomes back into the next recommendation |
| Indeed Career Scout | Conversational discovery, role exploration, resume help, interview practice, My Jobs tracking | Bring the market evidence, learning path, and proof-building output into the same transparent workflow |
| Teal | Job tracker, resume builder, job matcher | Prioritize one gap from a broader live market audit and connect it to learning and outcome diagnosis |
| Simplify | Job matches, autofill, tailoring, documents, and application tracking | Move beyond faster application throughput toward evidence quality and a learning loop |
| roadmap.sh | AI courses, guides, roadmaps, quizzes, and learning plans | Anchor the roadmap to the user's resume and a current target-role evidence set |
| Coursera Skills Tracks | Data-backed mapping between occupations, skills, and learning content | Make the individual job seeker's live applications and proof artifacts part of the loop |

## High-leverage product moves

### Fix now

1. Lead with a career evidence ledger, not a feature grid.
2. Show the provenance of each recommendation: resume evidence, audited job requirements, and why a gap was prioritized.
3. Keep learning paths project-based and connected to a concrete application artifact.
4. Let the outcome tracker change the next recommended move after enough personal results exist.
5. Use direct ATS sources and freshness cues as trust signals without overpromising that every posting is active.

### Build next

1. Connect a matched job's missing skill directly to a prefilled learning-path request.
2. Attach the resulting project deliverable back to saved jobs and tailored resume versions.
3. Add an explicit outcome-to-recommendation explanation after five or more logged outcomes.
4. Surface uncertainty and evidence counts instead of unexplained match percentages.

### Needs deeper validation

1. Whether users prefer one prioritized gap or a ranked top three.
2. Whether the strongest retention loop begins at job search, readiness audit, or learning-path generation.
3. Which proof artifact recruiters actually open: deployed demo, GitHub activity, written case study, or a compact evidence receipt.
